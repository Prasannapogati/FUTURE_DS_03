# FUTURE_DS_03 – Marketing Campaign Performance Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes marketing campaign performance and customer subscription conversion using Power BI and the Bank Marketing dataset.

## Objectives
- Analyze total customers and subscription conversion rate.
- Compare successful and unsuccessful subscriptions.
- Identify campaign performance trends.
- Analyze customer contact frequency and previous campaign outcomes.
- Provide actionable recommendations for improving marketing campaigns.

## Dashboard Pages
### 1. Overview
- Total Customers
- Conversion Rate
- Successful Subscriptions
- Unsuccessful Subscriptions
- Campaign-wise and job-wise subscription analysis
- Customer distribution by contact method

### 2. Campaign Analysis
- Monthly subscription trends
- Customer campaign contact frequency
- Previous campaign outcome analysis
- Interactive job, month, and contact slicers

### 3. Insights and Recommendations
- Subscription status analysis
- Previous campaign outcome analysis
- Business recommendations based on the findings

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX Measures
- Bank Marketing Dataset

## Key Insights
- Approximately 12% of customers subscribed to the term deposit.
- Marketing campaign outcomes vary across customer groups and previous campaign results.
- Campaign tracking and targeted customer communication can help improve future campaign performance.

## Deliverables
- Interactive Power BI dashboard
- Campaign performance analysis
- Data-driven business recommendations
